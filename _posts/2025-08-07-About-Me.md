# Welcome! My name is **Nguyen Tien Dung** but i prefer *Suiikawaii* 👋

I’m a **third-year student at USTH (Vietnam–France University)**. This blog is where I document a hands-on cybersecurity journey through **step-by-step CTF write-ups** and practical notes.

![USTH]({{ '/assets/images/aboutme/usth.jpg' | relative_url }})

Here you’ll find:
- Write-ups from **OverTheWire**, **TryHackMe**, **Hack The Box**, **picoCTF**, and various **CTF competitions**, plus more as I keep learning.
- Concise, reproducible walkthroughs and checklists I can revisit later.
- Occasional notes about how I study and build workflows at USTH.
- My project roadmap and long-term **cybersecurity career** goals.

Off-screen, I enjoy **listening to music**, **going to the gym**, **playing CTFs**, and **watching VTubers** — especially **Hoshimachi Suisei**.

## Why Hoshimachi Suisei? ⭐
![Hoshimachi Suisei]({{ '/assets/images/aboutme/suisei.gif' | relative_url }})

Suisei’s music is my study fuel during long labs and late-night CTFs. I love the mix of powerful vocals and crisp production — perfect for getting into flow. I’ll occasionally drop favorite tracks or live clips in posts so fellow fans can vibe along.

**Quick facts**
- VTuber & singer from **hololive (Gen 0)**. Debuted independently on **Mar 22, 2018**, later moved from INNK Music to the main hololive branch on **Dec 1, 2019**.  
- Albums: **Still Still Stellar** (2021) and **Specter** (2023).  
- First VTuber on **THE FIRST TAKE** (Jan 20, 2023) with “**Stellar Stellar**”.  
- Signature originals: **NEXT COLOR PLANET** (2020), **GHOST** (2021), **Stellar Stellar** (2021).  
- Fandom name: **Hoshiyomi** (“Stargazers”).
